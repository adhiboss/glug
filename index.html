01 / THE IDEA

More than a group.
A space to grow.

Free Software Movement Karnataka

Technology should empower people — not limit them.

Free Software Movement Karnataka (FSMK) is a registered not-for-profit organization working to create and spread awareness of free software technologies across different sections of society.

Driven by volunteers — including software engineers, students, academicians and government officials — FSMK works with college students to help them use, understand and contribute to free software.

A SIMPLE IDEA. FOUR FREEDOMS.

Free as in
freedom.

Free Software is software that respects the user's freedom. It is about control, transparency and the ability to learn from the tools we use.

01Run the program for any purpose.
02Study how it works and change it.
03Share copies with others.
04Share your improved versions.

Think “free” as in free speech, not free biryani.

03 / OUR CAMPUS

Rooted at JU-FET.
Open to everyone.

JAIN University Faculty of Engineering and Technology (JU-FET) brings together students, faculty and facilities that encourage learning beyond the classroom. With a focus on practical skills, research and student–mentor interaction, the campus offers a place for ideas to grow.

JAIN GLUG extends that spirit into the world of free software — creating opportunities to explore GNU/Linux, collaborate on projects, exchange ideas and learn from one another.

07 / CONTINUE THE CONVERSATION

Ideas worth discussing.

What is the purpose of a GLUG? ↗

A GLUG brings students together to learn about, use, discuss and contribute to free software and hardware.

How do we sustain a GLUG? ↗

Keep it active through regular sessions, shared projects, peer mentoring, open discussions and collaboration with the wider free software community.

How can I start a GLUG at my institution? ↗

Connect with an FSMK member, arrange an introductory session and work together to form and grow your campus community.
